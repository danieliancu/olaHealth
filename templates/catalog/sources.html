{% translate "Where the data comes from" %}

{% blocktranslate %}Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a collaborative, open database of food products, and from corrections sent by AskFoodo users and checked by our moderators. Each product page shows the source of every field and when it was last checked.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "AskFoodo only reads Open Food Facts; it never writes to it. Products appear in our catalogue when someone scans or types their barcode." %}

{% translate "Indicators" %}

  • {% translate "Nutri-Score and the processing level are shown as published by Open Food Facts, with the Nutri-Score algorithm version (2021 or 2023) when available. AskFoodo does not compute its own score." %}
  • {% translate "A Nutri-Score printed on the package is shown separately from the one computed by Open Food Facts." %}
  • {% translate "Each indicator shows whether data is available, missing, not applicable or conflicting, and whether it is up to date with Open Food Facts." %}
  • {% translate "When our moderators correct nutrition facts or ingredients, indicators computed from the earlier data are marked “not current” instead of being shown as valid." %}
  • {% translate "Missing information is never treated as zero, and the absence of listed allergens never means a product is allergen-free." %}
  • {% translate "Comparisons put values side by side on the same basis (per 100 g or per 100 ml) and never designate a winner." %}
  • {% translate "“Data completeness” measures how much key information is present. It is not a health rating." %}
